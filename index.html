Core loop

Prepare. Merge. Survive. Evolve.

Portal Siege separates tactical preparation from real-time combat. Build your formation, improve identical units through merging, then watch your choices collide with increasingly dangerous enemy compositions.

Recruit

Spend gold to summon units from the classes currently unlocked for your run or story progression.

Arrange

Place the active army on a 3×3 grid and use the reserve bench to prepare merges and manage tempo.

Fight

Units automatically target, move, attack, heal, and trigger researched abilities during each wave.

Research

Choose run upgrades, earn persistent essence, and unlock permanent improvements between attempts.

Progression systems

Build a run—and a legacy.

Portal Siege combines session decisions with persistent progression, while Story Mode uses missions, chapter bosses, currency, and unlocks to introduce systems over time.

Run Research

Develop damage, durability, economy, castle defenses, unit abilities, and enemy modifiers during the current run.

Resets each run

Permanent Research

Spend earned essence on persistent upgrades such as extra bench capacity, starting resources, and broader research access.

Story Mode

Advance through mission-based chapters, unlock classes and research branches, and face curated boss encounters.
